How this observatory works

The sky and the data lens show the same real objects. Select in one and they light up in the other.

Each mission retraces a discovery: read the briefing, lock in a prediction, then do the measurement the astronomers did. When it is done, new quantities unlock (distance, luminosity, temperature, radius, mass) and appear as axes for every dataset. Zoom out through the scale ladder on the sky as the chapters go from stars to galaxies.
