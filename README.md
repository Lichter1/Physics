## cGħ Lab

`cgh-lab/` is an interactive lab for building intuition about relativity, gravity and quantum mechanics by
changing c, G and ħ until their effects show up at human scale. Open `cgh-lab/index.html` in a browser.
See `cgh-lab/README.md` for how it works and `cgh-lab/RESEARCH.md` for a survey of existing tools.
