    The rule of the game. Only ratios without units are physical. Changing c or ħ alone also changes the fine-structure constant α = e²/4πε₀ħc, and with it every atom. This lab does what George Gamow did in Mr Tompkins: it keeps people, streets and footballs intact by decree and changes only how they move, fall and spread. The table shows what that decree hides.

    Slow-light street

    Ride down a street with clocks overhead and trams passing. Every lamp flashes at the same instant and every clock agrees, in the town's own frame. What reaches your eyes is another matter.

    What to try

    • Stand still. The lamps flash in a ripple that runs away from you, and far clocks read earlier than near ones. Nothing is out of sync; the light is late.
    • Push the effort up. The street ahead stretches away and bends, things beside you swing into view, and the clock ahead runs fast. Turn around and it runs slow.
    • Switch to “What is there”. That is the street in your frame at this instant: simply squashed along your motion. You never see it that way, because light from its far end left earlier.
    • Ride out and back, then compare your watch with the town clock.

The tower uses a uniform field (an accelerating frame); the mission uses circular equatorial orbits around a Kerr black hole. The quantum scenes solve the Schrödinger equation for one particle with no decoherence, which is the generous assumption a football would need. Where all three constants matter at once there is no accepted theory, and the lab says so instead of guessing.
